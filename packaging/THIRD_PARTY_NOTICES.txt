Exhume desktop app: third-party software

Exhume itself is MIT licensed (see LICENSE). The downloadable app also bundles the software
below. Each package's own licence text ships inside the app, in its *.dist-info folder; Python's
licence (which also covers the libraries Python is built with: OpenSSL, libffi, SQLite, zlib,
bzip2, XZ, expat, mpdecimal, Tcl/Tk) ships as LICENSE.txt.
